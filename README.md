## Finance systems, grounded in accounting

I'm Marc Pios, also known as Kristian. I'm a Systems Accountant: fractional finance management and bank general-ledger work, plus the software that removes the manual steps around them.

Automation only earns its place when it stays auditable. In practice: defined business rules, a named approver, a written record of what changed, and a ledger that still balances after the run. When a coding agent drafts part of a workflow, the accounting rules and the approval step are written down and checked by a person.

## Selected work

Employment facts and project evidence are kept separate. Each project links to the repository that documents it.

**[marcpios.com](https://www.marcpios.com)**: the portfolio itself, a six-page ledger built with Next.js and TypeScript. Typography, motion, and the receipt-to-ledger story are authored, not themed; the site ships its own tests for routing, structured data, accessibility, and discovery.

**[Receipt to ledger](https://www.marcpios.com/work/odoo-intake-pipeline)**: a case study of an intake pipeline where AI proposes fields from a receipt, deterministic rules do the arithmetic, and posting stays a human step. Untagged captures are dropped and verified rows are never rewritten.

**[n8n Workflow Library](https://n8n.io/creators/kristian/)**: accounting workflow examples, including routing Telegram quote requests to Xero and disbursement requests to Trello.

**[Discord to n8n Bridge](https://github.com/kristianism/discord-n8n-bridge)**: a TypeScript service that holds a Discord Gateway connection, normalizes new and edited messages into a stable JSON envelope, and delivers them to an n8n webhook with idempotency keys, bounded delivery, retries, and guild and channel allowlists.
